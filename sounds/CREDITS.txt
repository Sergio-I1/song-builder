Instrument sounds: Fluid R3 GM soundfont by Frank Wen, released under the
Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 license (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Rendered to MP3 by Benjamin Gleitzman's midi-js-soundfonts project
(https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts, MIT license, see MIDI-JS-SOUNDFONTS-LICENSE.txt).
Notes were trimmed to each instrument's playing range for Song Builder.

Drum sounds (sounds/drums.json): kick, snare, hi-hat and percussion samples from the
GoogleChromeLabs web-audio-samples project (mld-drum-sampler demo),
https://github.com/GoogleChromeLabs/web-audio-samples, Apache License 2.0.

Acoustic guitar and strings (sounds/mk_acoustic_guitar_steel.json, sounds/mk_string_ensemble_1.json):
Musyng Kite soundfont, as rendered to MP3 by the midi-js-soundfonts project
(https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts), released under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 license (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
These two files were trimmed to each instrument's playing range for Song Builder and,
as the license requires, are shared under that same CC BY-SA 3.0 license.
