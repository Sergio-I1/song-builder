Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 license (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Rendered to MP3 by Benjamin Gleitzman's midi-js-soundfonts project
(https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts, MIT license, see MIDI-JS-SOUNDFONTS-LICENSE.txt).
