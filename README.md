# Song Builder

Drag-and-drop song sketchpad: arrange song parts, pick chords (in-key, any chord, or a 12-note keyboard), choose a strum pattern, and see what notes you sing.

Open it: https://sergio-i1.github.io/song-builder/

## Versions

Each version keeps its own link, so older ones never change:
- **v1** (Nov 2026): first version: https://sergio-i1.github.io/song-builder/v1/
- **v2** (Nov 2026): adds the Sing with chords screen (record over the song; voice chart on top, chords lined up underneath): https://sergio-i1.github.io/song-builder/v2/
- **v3** (Nov 2026): volume sliders for chords and voice, notched pitch sliders (one half step at a time), and a popular-songs list: https://sergio-i1.github.io/song-builder/v3/
- **v4** (Nov 2026): 12 instruments you can layer, songs load their own instruments and strum, a drag-and-drop strum pattern builder, a custom chord bank, and gentler drag scrolling: https://sergio-i1.github.io/song-builder/v4/
- **v5** (Nov 2026): time signatures (4/4, 3/4, 6/8, 12/8), busier strum and picking patterns for slow songs, and 83 popular songs with genre filters: https://sergio-i1.github.io/song-builder/v5/
- **v6** (Nov 2026): real recorded instrument sounds, full song layouts (intro, verses, choruses, bridge, outro in order), two chords per bar, checked tempos, and a Song pitch control: https://sergio-i1.github.io/song-builder/v6/

Instrument sounds: Fluid R3 GM soundfont by Frank Wen (CC BY 3.0), via midi-js-soundfonts. See `sounds/CREDITS.txt`.

## Also in this repo: Lecture Room

An AI professor that teaches any lecture back to you like a video call. Paste notes or a transcript, or upload PDF slides or photos of slides. It cuts the lecture into 2-minute parts, talks through each one in plain words while drawing on a whiteboard, and stops after each part to ask if you have questions. Raise your hand any time to ask something. There is a podcast mode (no check-ins) and a built-in demo class on voltage, current and resistance.

Open it: https://sergio-i1.github.io/song-builder/lecture-room/

- Pick from five teachers: Prof. Maya (American woman), Prof. Marcus (American man), Prof. Eleanor (British woman), Prof. James (British man) and Prof. Priya (Indian English woman). They pause naturally between ideas, and their faces move as they talk.
- Premium voice (GitHub page): the most human-sounding voices, from OpenAI, with each teacher's accent and a warm teaching style. Add your own OpenAI API key in Settings; it costs a few cents per class.
- Each teacher sits in their own office, blinks, breathes, nods, glances at the whiteboard and holds up a marker while drawing. Tell the teacher your name and they'll use it.
- On the GitHub page you can also turn on Studio voice in Settings: a much more human-sounding AI voice that runs in your browser for free (one-time ~90 MB download; best on a computer).
- The demo works right away. To teach your own lectures on the GitHub page, add your own Anthropic API key in Settings (saved only in your browser).
- The Claude version uses your Claude account instead, no key needed (private link, only Sergio can open it unless shared): https://claude.ai/artifact/BtA2tgs9NvjuwwX1DMpKBe
